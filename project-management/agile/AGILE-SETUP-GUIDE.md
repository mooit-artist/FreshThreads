## 🎯 Agile Framework Adaptation

### **Sprint Structure (1-Week Cycles)**

- **Monday:** Sprint Planning & Product Backlog Refinement
- **Tuesday-Thursday:** Sprint Development/Implementation
- **Friday:** Sprint Review, Retrospective & Next Sprint Planning
- **Weekend:** Research, creative work, and customer validation

### **Agile Ceremonies (Solo Adaptation)**

- **📋 Sprint Planning:** Define sprint goal and select backlog items
- **🔄 Daily Standup:** Morning review (What did I do? What will I do? Blockers?)
- **👀 Sprint Review:** Demo completed work, validate with criteria
- **🔄 Retrospective:** What worked? What didn't? What to improve?

## 📊 Sprint Planning & Estimation

### **Sprint Capacity Planning:**

- **Available Hours per Sprint:** 20-25 hours (part-time)
- **Story Point Velocity Target:** 10-15 points per sprint
- **Story Point Guidelines:**
  - **1 point:** 1-2 hours (simple configuration)
  - **2 points:** 3-4 hours (moderate setup)
  - **3 points:** 5-6 hours (complex configuration)
  - **5 points:** 8-10 hours (multi-day project)
  - **8 points:** 12-16 hours (full week effort)

### **Sprint Planning Process:**

1. **Review Product Backlog** - Prioritize by business value
2. **Select Sprint Goal** - One clear objective per sprint
3. **Choose User Stories** - Based on capacity and dependencies
4. **Break Down Tasks** - Identify specific implementation steps
5. **Estimate Story Points** - Use planning poker (solo adaptation)
