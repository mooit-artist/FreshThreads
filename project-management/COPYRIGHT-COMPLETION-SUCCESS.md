## 📋 Next Steps - Trademark Considerations

### Copyright vs Trademark Differences

**Copyright (✅ COMPLETED):**

- Protects the **artistic expression** of your logo
- Automatic protection upon creation
- Covers unauthorized copying/reproduction
- Duration: Life + 70 years (or 95 years for company works)
- Cost: $65 one-time fee

**Trademark (Under Consideration):**

- Protects the **commercial use** as a brand identifier
- Requires actual use in commerce
- Covers unauthorized use in similar business categories
- Duration: Renewable every 10 years (potentially forever)
- Cost: $250-$400+ per class

### Trademark Analysis for Fresh Threads LLC

**Pros of Getting Trademark:**

1. **Brand Protection** - Prevents competitors from using similar logos in apparel
2. **National Registration** - Stronger legal position than state-only
3. **® Symbol Rights** - Professional credibility marker
4. **Licensing Revenue** - Can license trademark to others
5. **Business Valuation** - Trademarked brands worth more

**Cons/Considerations:**

1. **Higher Cost** - $250-$400+ per classification
2. **Use Requirement** - Must be actively using in commerce
3. **Maintenance** - Renewal fees every 10 years
4. **Search Required** - Need professional trademark search first
5. **Application Complexity** - More complex than copyright

### Recommended Trademark Strategy

**Phase 1: Now (Optional)**

- **USPTO TEAS Basic Application:** $350 per class
- **Classification 025:** Clothing (your T-shirt business)
- **Classification 035:** Retail services (if you plan online store)

**Phase 2: After Revenue (Recommended)**

- Wait until you have actual sales/revenue
- Stronger trademark application with proof of commerce
- Better ROI when business is established
