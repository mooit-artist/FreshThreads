## 🚨 **POTENTIAL ISSUES & SOLUTIONS**

### **Common Challenges:**

1. **No EIN Yet?**
   - Solution: Apply for EIN first at [irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)
   - Takes 5-10 minutes online

2. **Business Not Legally Formed?**
   - Solution: File LLC formation with your state first
   - Many states allow online filing (1-7 days)

3. **No Business Address?**
   - Solution: Use home address initially
   - Can update later when you have commercial space

4. **Credit Check Concerns?**
   - American Express typically does business and personal credit checks
   - Have good personal credit or consider alternatives
