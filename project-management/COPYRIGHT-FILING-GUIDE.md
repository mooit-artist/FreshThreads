## 🚀 **Step-by-Step Copyright Filing Process**

### **Step 1: Go to Copyright.gov**

**URL:** https://www.copyright.gov/registration/

Click **"Register a Work"** → **"Log In to eCO"**

### **Step 2: Create Account (if needed)**

- **Account Type:** Individual or Organization
- **Organization Name:** Fresh Threads LLC
- **Email:** procurement@freshthreadsllc.com (your business email)
- **Password:** Use a strong password

### **Step 3: Start New Registration**

**Click:** "Register a New Claim"

**Work Type:** Select **"Work of the Visual Arts"**

- This covers logos, graphics, artwork

### **Step 4: Fill Out Application**

#### **Title Information:**

- **Title of Work:** "Fresh Threads LLC Logo"
- **Previous Title:** Leave blank
- **Publication:** Select "No" (unpublished work)

#### **Author Information:**

- **Author:** Fresh Threads LLC
- **Work Made for Hire:** Yes (if you created it for the company)
- **Citizenship:** United States
- **Year of Birth:** Leave blank for organization

#### **Claimant Information:**

- **Claimant:** Fresh Threads LLC
- **Address:** Your business address
- **Transfer Statement:** "Work made for hire" (if applicable)

#### **Rights and Permissions:**

- **Rights and Permissions Contact:** procurement@freshthreadsllc.com
- **Description:** "Company logo for Fresh Threads LLC apparel business"

### **Step 5: Upload Your Logo File**

- **File:** Fresh_ThreadsLLCLogo.png
- **File Size:** Must be under 500MB (your 1024x1024 PNG is perfect)
- **Format:** PNG is acceptable

### **Step 6: Review and Pay**

- **Filing Fee:** $45 (Standard Application)
- **Payment:** Credit card, debit card, or electronic check
- **Processing Time:** 3-6 months for certificate
