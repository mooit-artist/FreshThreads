```

**Option B: Created as work-for-hire for the LLC**

```

## 🏛️ **Registered Agent Considerations**

### **Copyright Filing: NO REGISTERED AGENT NEEDED**

- ✅ File directly with Copyright Office
- ✅ Use your business address
- ✅ Save $150-300 in agent fees
- ✅ Faster processing

### **When Registered Agents ARE Required:**

- State business formation documents
- Annual reports and state filings
- Legal service of process (lawsuits)
- Some complex trademark disputes

### **When Registered Agents MAY Help:**

- Multiple state business operations
- Privacy protection (address shielding)
- Legal compliance management
- Complex IP portfolio management
