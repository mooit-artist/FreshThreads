## 🎯 **Logo Protection Options**

### **1. TRADEMARK (Recommended for Business Logo)**

**What it protects:** Your business name, logo, and brand identity
**Duration:** 10 years, renewable indefinitely
**Cost:** $225-$400 per class (USPTO fees)
**Timeline:** 8-12 months for approval

**Why Trademark > Copyright for Logos:**

- ✅ **Business Protection:** Prevents competitors from using similar logos
- ✅ **Brand Exclusivity:** Exclusive rights to "Fresh Threads" in apparel
- ✅ **Market Protection:** Stops trademark infringement in your industry
- ✅ **Legal Standing:** Strong enforcement rights in court
- ✅ **Business Value:** Increases company valuation

### **2. COPYRIGHT (Automatic, but Limited)**

**What it protects:** The artistic expression of your logo
**Duration:** Your lifetime + 70 years (or 95 years for corporate work)
**Cost:** $45-$65 for formal registration
**Timeline:** 3-6 months

**Limitations for Business Logos:**

- ❌ **No Business Name Protection:** Doesn't protect "Fresh Threads LLC"
- ❌ **Limited Commercial Value:** Weak protection against competitors
- ❌ **Artistic Only:** Only protects creative expression, not business use

## 🏛️ **USPTO Trademark Application Process**

### **Step 1: Trademark Search**

```bash
# Free search at USPTO database
https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/

# Search terms:
- "Fresh Threads"
- "Fresh Threads LLC"
- Similar clothing/apparel trademarks
```

### **Step 2: Choose Trademark Class**

**Class 025:** Clothing, footwear, headwear (your primary class)

- T-shirts, apparel, fashion accessories
- **Cost:** $225-$400 depending on filing method

**Optional Additional Classes:**

- **Class 035:** Retail services, online sales
- **Class 042:** Website services, e-commerce platform

### **Step 3: File Application**

**TEAS Plus:** $225 per class (cheapest, more restrictions)
**TEAS Standard:** $350 per class (more flexibility)

### **Required Information:**

- Business name: "Fresh Threads LLC"
- Logo file: Fresh_ThreadsLLCLogo.png
- Business address
- Goods/services description
- Date of first use (when you started using the logo)
