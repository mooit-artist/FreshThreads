## 🚨 **POTENTIAL BLOCKERS & SOLUTIONS**

### **Common Issues:**

1. **Bank Verification Delays** (1-2 days)
   - Solution: Start immediately, use backup payment method

2. **Identity Verification Required**
   - Solution: Have ID and business docs ready

3. **HTTPS Required for Apple/Google Pay**
   - Solution: Ensure website has SSL certificate

4. **Business Verification**
   - Solution: Have EIN and business registration ready
