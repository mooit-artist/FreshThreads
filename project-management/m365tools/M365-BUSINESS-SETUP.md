## 🚀 Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. **Start Microsoft 365 Business Signup**

- Go to: [Microsoft 365 Business](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business)
- Click **"Get Microsoft 365"** → **"Business Basic"**
- Select **"Buy now"** ($6.00/month)

### 2. **Account Creation**

- **Important:** Use a **NEW** Microsoft account (not your personal one)
- Email for setup: Use a temporary email or create `bryan.temp@gmail.com`
- Password: Use strong business password (store in password manager)
- **Business Name:** Fresh Threads LLC
- **Size:** Just me (1-300 people)

### 3. **Domain Setup**

- When asked "What domain do you want to use?"
- Select **"Use a domain you already own"**
- Enter: **freshthreadsllc.com**
- **Important:** You'll need to verify domain ownership

### 4. **Domain Verification Process**

- Microsoft will provide a **TXT record** to add to your domain
- Log into your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
- Add the TXT record to DNS settings
- Wait 15-30 minutes for propagation
- Return to Microsoft to verify
