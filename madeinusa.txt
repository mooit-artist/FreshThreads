Great news, Bryan — both Printify and Printful offer options that can support a “Made in the USA” label, but with some important caveats:
🇺🇸 Printify: “Assembled in the USA” Products
Printify has a dedicated catalog of products labeled “Assembled in the USA”, which means:
The printing and final assembly occur in U.S.-based facilities
The blank products may be sourced internationally
Brands like Bella+Canvas, Bayside, and US Blanks offer some items that are cut, sewn, or dyed in the U.S.
🧠 Tip: You’ll need to research each print provider and product SKU to verify origin. For example:
Bella+Canvas 3001U is one of the few shirts fully made in the U.S.
Some mugs and accessories are printed in the U.S. but manufactured abroad
🇺🇸 Printful: U.S.-Based Fulfillment with Select Domestic Products
Printful operates multiple fulfillment centers in the U.S., and they offer a curated selection of products that are partly or fully made in America:
Products are printed, embroidered, or sewn in U.S. facilities
Blank goods are sourced from suppliers like American Apparel, Royal Apparel, and Bella+Canvas
Printful emphasizes ethical sourcing and sustainability, even if not all products are 100% U.S.-made
🧠 Tip: Use Printful’s Apparel Brand Guide to identify which items qualify for “Made in USA” marketing.
✅ Labeling Guidance
To stay compliant and transparent:
Use “Printed in the USA” or “Assembled in the USA” unless the entire supply chain is domestic
Consider “Made from USA-grown cotton” for brands like Comfort Colors
Avoid “Made in USA” unless you’ve verified full domestic origin — including fabric, thread, and labor
