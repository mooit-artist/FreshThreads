**Sprint 1 - Issue #26: Research T-shirt suppliers (2 story points)**
**Goal:** Identify and evaluate print-on-demand partners for Fresh Threads LLC

## 🎯 Print-on-Demand Supplier Evaluation

### Top Print-on-Demand Services:

### 1. **Printful** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

- **Pricing:** $8.95-$13.95 per shirt
- **Quality:** Premium, soft feel
- **Shipping:** 2-7 business days
- **Integration:** Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, manual
- **Pros:** High quality, reliable, good mockups
- **Cons:** Higher prices
- **Best For:** Premium positioning ($24.99 retail)

### 2. **Printify** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

- **Pricing:** $4.99-$8.99 per shirt
- **Quality:** Good, multiple print partners
- **Shipping:** 3-10 business days
- **Integration:** Multiple platforms
- **Pros:** Lower cost, multiple suppliers
- **Cons:** Quality varies by partner
- **Best For:** Competitive pricing ($19.99 retail)

### 3. **Gooten** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

- **Pricing:** $6.50-$10.50 per shirt
- **Quality:** Good, consistent
- **Shipping:** 3-7 business days
- **Integration:** API-first, good for developers
- **Pros:** Developer-friendly, good API
- **Cons:** Less marketing support
- **Best For:** Tech-savvy businesses

### 4. **Teespring (Spring)** ⭐⭐⭐

- **Pricing:** $7.00-$12.00 per shirt
- **Quality:** Good
- **Shipping:** 4-14 business days
- **Integration:** Built-in storefront
- **Pros:** All-in-one platform
- **Cons:** Less customization
- **Best For:** Quick start, built-in audience

## 🏆 Supplier Selection Criteria

### Quality Factors:

- [ ] Fabric quality (cotton blend, softness)
- [ ] Print durability (wash resistance)
- [ ] Color accuracy
- [ ] Size consistency

### Business Factors:

- [ ] Pricing structure
- [ ] Shipping speed and costs
- [ ] Integration capabilities
- [ ] Customer service
- [ ] Return/refund policies

### Technical Factors:

- [ ] File format requirements
- [ ] Print area limitations
- [ ] Color limitations
- [ ] Setup fees

## 🔍 Detailed Evaluation

### Printful Deep Dive:

**Products:** Bella+Canvas 3001, Gildan 64000, Next Level 3600
**Print Methods:** DTG (Direct-to-Garment), Embroidery

**Print Areas:** Front (12"×16"), Back (12"×16"), Left Chest (3.5"×3.5")

**Products:** Multiple suppliers (Gooten, SPOD, etc.)

**Pricing:** Varies by print partner
**Integration:** Good platform support
**Quality Control:** Depends on chosen supplier
