Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the US. Express shipping (2-3 days) and international shipping options are also available.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in original condition. Custom designs are non-returnable unless there's a printing defect.
Can I submit my own designs?
Absolutely! We love working with independent artists. Email us at artists@freshthreadsllc.com with your portfolio and collaboration ideas.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes, we offer competitive wholesale pricing for bulk orders (50+ items). Contact our business team for a custom quote.
What materials do you use?
We use premium, sustainably-sourced materials including organic cotton, recycled polyester, and eco-friendly inks for all our printing.
