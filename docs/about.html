About Fresh Threads

Purpose in Every Thread

Purpose in Every Thread: FreshThreads was born from a desire to make shirts that matter. We use premium, natural materials not just for comfort and quality—but to support a bigger idea: that clothing can be a vehicle for change.

Our designs carry a creative edge, but they're rooted in purpose. Each shirt is part of a limited-run collection tied to causes we believe in. It's not just about fashion—it's about wearing your values, sparking conversation, and supporting impact through intentional design.

Annika Jorgensen

Owner, Customer Service
