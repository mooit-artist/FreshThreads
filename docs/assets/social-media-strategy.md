# FreshThreads Social Media Strategy

## 🎯 **Brand Identity**
- **Mission**: Premium print-on-demand apparel with unique, fresh designs
- **Target Audience**: 18-35 year olds interested in fashion, custom apparel, and self-expression
- **Brand Voice**: Fresh, trendy, confident, inclusive, creative

## 📱 **Platform Strategy**

### **Instagram (@freshthreadsllc)**
- **Content Types**: Product photos, behind-the-scenes design process, customer photos, style inspiration
- **Posting Schedule**: 1-2 posts daily, 3-5 stories daily
- **Key Hashtags**: #FreshThreads #CustomApparel #PrintOnDemand #StreetStyle #Fashion

### **TikTok (@freshthreadsllc)**
- **Content Types**: Design process videos, styling tips, trending fashion challenges, customer reactions
- **Posting Schedule**: 3-5 videos per week
- **Trends to Follow**: Fashion trends, design process, "outfit of the day"

### **Facebook (FreshThreads LLC)**
- **Content Types**: Product announcements, customer stories, company updates, community building
- **Posting Schedule**: 3-4 posts per week
- **Focus**: Community building and customer service

### **Pinterest (FreshThreads LLC)**
- **Content Types**: Design inspiration boards, style guides, seasonal collections
- **Boards**: "Design Inspiration", "Street Style", "Custom Tees", "Hoodie Styles"
- **Focus**: Drive traffic to website through visual discovery

### **YouTube (FreshThreads LLC)**
- **Content Types**: Design tutorials, brand story, customer testimonials, fashion hauls
- **Schedule**: 1-2 videos per week when launched

## 🎨 **Content Calendar Ideas**

### **Pre-Launch Content**
1. **Behind-the-scenes**: Design process, team introductions
2. **Teasers**: Sneak peeks of upcoming designs
3. **Countdown**: Building anticipation for launch
4. **Community**: Asking followers what designs they want to see

### **Post-Launch Content**
1. **Product showcases**: New designs, seasonal collections
2. **Customer features**: User-generated content, styling photos
3. **Tutorials**: How to style pieces, care instructions
4. **Trends**: Fashion trend breakdowns, seasonal styles

## 📊 **Key Metrics to Track**
- Follower growth rate
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through rate to website
- Conversion rate from social to sales
- User-generated content mentions

## 🤝 **Engagement Strategy**
- Respond to comments within 2-4 hours
- Use polls and questions in stories
- Collaborate with micro-influencers (1K-100K followers)
- Create branded hashtags for campaigns
- Share customer photos and testimonials

## 🎁 **Campaign Ideas**
1. **#FreshThreadsChallenge**: Styling challenge on TikTok
2. **Design Contest**: Let followers vote on new designs
3. **Customer Spotlight**: Feature customer photos weekly
4. **Seasonal Campaigns**: Back-to-school, holiday collections
5. **Influencer Partnerships**: Send free products for authentic reviews

## 📧 **Cross-Platform Integration**
- Newsletter signups from social media
- Social media links on website
- Consistent branding across all platforms
- Cross-promote content between platforms
- Use social proof on website (customer photos from social)

## 🚀 **Launch Strategy**
1. **Week 1**: Announce launch date, build anticipation
2. **Launch Day**: Go live across all platforms, special launch discount
3. **Week 2-4**: Customer spotlights, design stories, community building
4. **Month 2+**: Regular content schedule, seasonal campaigns

## 📈 **Growth Tactics**
- Partner with fashion bloggers and influencers
- Run targeted social media ads
- Use trending hashtags strategically
- Engage with fashion and design communities
- Collaborate with complementary brands
- Run contests and giveaways

Remember: Authenticity and quality content perform better than frequent posting!
