# 🧾 Business Setup Checklist (Day 1)

**Goal:** Launch foundational tools for a one-man print-on-demand business
**Status:** 🚧 In Progress
**Owner:** @bryan

---

## 🏦 Business Banking

- [ ] Open **American Express® Business Checking**
  - [ ] Gather EIN or SSN
  - [ ] Confirm business name & structure
  - [ ] Provide business address & contact info
  - [ ] Apply online: [Amex Business Checking](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/banking/business-checking/)

---

## 💳 Payment Platforms

- [ ] Create **Stripe** account
  - [ ] Add business info
  - [ ] Link Amex Checking
  - [ ] Verify identity
  - [ ] Set up products or connect to storefront

- [ ] Create **PayPal Business** account
  - [ ] Link Amex Checking
  - [ ] Enable PayPal Checkout, Venmo, PayPal Credit

---

## 🧾 Accounting Setup

- [ ] Sign up for **QuickBooks Online**
  - [ ] Choose Simple Start ($38) or Essentials ($75)
  - [ ] Add business info
  - [ ] Link Amex Checking
  - [ ] Set up categories: income, expenses, fees

---

## 🔄 Stripe → QuickBooks Integration

- [ ] Choose integration tool
  - [ ] Acodei Scale100 ($12/month) → [acodei.com](https://acodei.com)
  - [ ] OR Synder Basic ($52/month) → [synder.com](https://synder.com)

- [ ] Connect Stripe to QuickBooks
  - [ ] Enable auto-sync for transactions, fees, refunds, payouts

---

## 📦 Storefront Prep

- [ ] Choose platform: Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy, Gumroad
- [ ] Connect Stripe and PayPal
- [ ] Add first products (mockups, descriptions, pricing)
- [ ] Set up shipping profiles and tax settings

---

## 📈 Financial Tracking

- [ ] Log initial expenses in QuickBooks
- [ ] Create categories for:
  - [ ] Software subscriptions
  - [ ] Marketing
  - [ ] Product costs
  - [ ] Payment processing fees

---

## 🧠 Optional: Loan Readiness

- [ ] Keep Amex Checking active and funded
- [ ] Track all revenue/expenses in QuickBooks
- [ ] Avoid mixing personal and business funds
- [ ] Open Amex High-Yield Savings once revenue starts
