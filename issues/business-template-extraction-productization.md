## 🚨 **Risk Assessment**

### **High Risk**

1. **Market Saturation:** Many business template products exist
   - **Mitigation:** Focus on specific niche (print-on-demand) and quality

2. **Technical Complexity:** Some components may be too complex to template
   - **Mitigation:** Start with simpler components, build complexity gradually

### **Medium Risk**

3. **Support Burden:** Customers may need extensive technical support
   - **Mitigation:** Create comprehensive documentation and video tutorials

4. **Legal Compliance:** Templates must work across different jurisdictions
   - **Mitigation:** Include legal disclaimers, focus on general guidance

### **Low Risk**

5. **Competition:** Large companies may create competing products
   - **Mitigation:** Focus on personalized service and entrepreneur community
